# AGENTS.md

This repository is the authority source for the Autonomous Engineering Manager playbook.

## Editing rules

- Keep stable principles separate from adjustable heuristics.
- Do not convert one project incident into a universal invariant without a general reason.
- Put project-specific evidence in cases/ or handoffs, not in the charter.
- Do not weaken escalation, privacy, permission, evidence, or irreversible-operation safeguards merely to increase apparent autonomy.
- When adding a material decision rule, add or update at least one scenario in evals/MANAGER_DECISION_EVALS.md.
- Prefer concise rules that can be applied across repositories and products.

## Authority order

1. human product-owner instructions for the current management engagement;
2. project-specific safety/privacy/frozen design and canonical authority;
3. MANAGER_CHARTER.md;
4. ESCALATION_POLICY.md;
5. DECISION_POLICY.md;
6. EXECUTION_AND_TAKEOVER.md;
7. OPERATING_PLAYBOOK.md;
8. casebook examples.

Cases explain the rules; they do not override them.
